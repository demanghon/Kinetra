# 08: Mobile Client Application (Android & iOS — React Native JSI + Skia 120Hz + On-Device Vision/Voice AI + Rust FFI) (`client-app`)

**What to build:** The high-fluidity native mobile application (`src/client-app`) built with **React Native (New Architecture JSI / TypeScript)** for Android and iOS ([ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-react-native-jsi-skia-and-on-device-edge-ai.md)), integrating our Rust physics engines (`imu-engine`, `ballistic-core`) via native ARM64 FFI, zero-copy $60\text{–}240\text{ FPS}$ camera capture (`react-native-vision-camera` v4) with on-device MediaPipe/LiteRT swing pose estimation, on-device conversational voice STT/TTS (`sherpa-onnx` / `whisper.rn` + `Piper Neural TTS`), live BLE (`react-native-ble-plx`) battery & $50\text{ mA}$ charging progress widget (`charging_state`, `estimated_minutes_to_full`, `SWITCH_OFF_DURING_CHARGE_WARNING`), automatic club selection on `mount_slot_id` change, and $120\text{ Hz}$ synchronized video + Skia telemetry curves (`Impact Clipping Signature` zoom + `Toe/Center/Heel` face map) + 3D trajectory rendering (`@react-three/fiber`).

**Blocked by:** 06: 3D Ballistic Integrator (RK4 6-DOF Magnus), Hybrid Fusion, `tour_ball` vs `range_ball` & `Dry Swing` (`ballistic-core`), 07: Interactive Voice (TTS) & Video Bag Scanner, Auto-ID Mount Binding & Single-Club Update (`bag-scanner`)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Integrates native Rust FFI (`imu-engine`, `ballistic-core`), BLE (`react-native-ble-plx`), zero-copy $60\text{–}240\text{ FPS}$ camera (`react-native-vision-camera` v4), on-device MediaPipe/LiteRT swing pose analysis, and on-device voice STT/TTS (`sherpa-onnx` / `whisper.rn`) on Android and iOS ([ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-react-native-jsi-skia-and-on-device-edge-ai.md)).
- [ ] Displays real-time sensor battery level, remaining play hours, live charging progress (`0%` to `100%` with `estimated_minutes_to_full`), and `SWITCH_OFF_DURING_CHARGE_WARNING` alert (`REQ-API-003`).
- [ ] Synchronizes the video player, $120\text{ Hz}$ Skia telemetry curves (speed, shaft deflection, $\pm 16\,g$ clipping & $\Delta \omega_z$ face strike map), and 3D flight scene (`@react-three/fiber`) within $\le 1\text{ frame}$ around $t = 0$ (`REQ-API-002`).
