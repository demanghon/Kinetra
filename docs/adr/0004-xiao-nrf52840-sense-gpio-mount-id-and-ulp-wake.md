# 0004 — Seeed XIAO BLE nRF52840 Sense Hardware Baseline, 4-Bit GPIO Auto-ID Mount & Wake-on-Use BLE Power Management

We standardize the GeoTrick IMU hardware on the **Seeed Studio XIAO BLE nRF52840 Sense (`102010469`)** powered by an **$85\text{ mAh}$ LiPo (`L301525`)** with master switch (`ESP101`), using its onboard **6-DoF `LSM6DS3TR-C`** ($\pm 16\,g$ accelerometer + $\pm 2000^\circ/\text{s}$ gyroscope, no magnetometer) and onboard **`MP34DT05-A` PDM microphone**. Each club's `Keyed Butt-Cap Mount` encodes its `mount_slot_id` (`1..14`) via a passive **4-bit binary wire jumper (`D0..D3` + `GND`)** with internal pull-ups, and the firmware disables the BLE radio whenever the club is unmounted or motionless, waking automatically on mount insertion (`GPIOTE PORT`) or physical club pick-up (`LSM6DS3TR-C INT1` hardware wake-up).

## Context & Trade-offs

1. **6-DoF (`LSM6DS3TR-C`) instead of 9-DoF**: The XIAO BLE nRF52840 Sense does not include a magnetometer. Eliminating magnetometer fusion is actually advantageous in golf environments (steel shafts and metal driving range bays distort magnetic heading). Azimuth reference is locked via the mechanically keyed butt-cap mount and the `Address Pose Tare` aligned with the camera target line.
2. **4-Bit Binary GPIO Auto-ID (`D0..D3` + `GND`) over NFC or Analog Resistor**: The nRF52840's internal NFC peripheral only supports passive Tag emulation (not NFC reader mode). Using 4 GPIO pins with internal pull-ups wired to `GND` inside each club's mount requires zero extra electronic components (only jumper wires in the mount), is immune to contact resistance variation under humidity, detects mount lock state (`code != 0b1111`), and identifies up to 14 clubs (`0b0001` to `0b1110`) instantaneously upon insertion.
3. **Wake-on-Use BLE Power Optimization for the $85\text{ mAh}$ LiPo**: Continuous BLE transmission + 1 kHz IMU + PDM mic draws $\sim 12\text{ mA}$ ($\sim 6\text{ h}$ battery life). By turning off the BLE radio when unmounted (`0b1111`, $\sim 15\,\mu\text{A}$) or when the club sits motionless for $>20\text{ s}$ ($\sim 25\,\mu\text{A}$ with `LSM6DS3TR-C` hardware Wake-on-Motion on `INT1`), and enabling BLE + 1 kHz capture only when the golfer picks up or mounts the club, effective battery life increases to $>25\text{ hours}$ of active play.
